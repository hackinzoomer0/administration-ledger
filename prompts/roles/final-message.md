FINAL MESSAGE FORMAT (mandatory; the phone notification and the final reply use exactly this format; Use short paragraphs. Send it with PushNotification inside <routine_summary> tags. Plain text only, no markdown tables (they do not render on a phone); use short dash bullets and blank lines between sections. Write in plain words: no issue codes, no internal jargon like "Stage 2", "passes" or "propagation" except inside an error line that needs them. Structure:
 Line 1 (the banner, under 140 characters): the single most consequential development stated as its net effect, or if the run skipped work it could have done or had a recall miss, begin with "INCOMPLETE:" or "Missed items:" followed by the most important one. Source failures (a page that errored, a tool limit) do NOT make the banner INCOMPLETE; they are informational and go in the section below.
 Blank line, then the heading "ERRORS AND INCOMPLETE ITEMS". One dash bullet per item in this form: "- <what the item was> | <what it covers in one sentence> | Why not done: <the three routes tried and why each failed, or the specific hard block>". List source and tool failures first, each with its exact error, so the reviewer sees every failure. These are informational only: they never change the next run's window. Include here: every checklist item not "done", every deviation, every blocked source that mattered, every gap with its three routes, every method-audit miss (what was missed, which source or query should have caught it, the proposed fix), and the repeat-miss escalation if any. If there are no misses, include one bullet: "- Search-method audit: no misses (recall sample N of N)". If there are no errors at all besides that bullet, say "None." before it.
 Blank line, then the heading "CONSEQUENTIAL NEWS". One dash bullet per item, at most 8, ordered by consequence, covering every new or revised item of weight 3 or higher plus any weight 2 item that changes a pending decision: "- <what actually happened, in one plain sentence, with the date and source outlet> | Consequence for what we track: <which factions or issues it moves, in which direction, and how confident (documented, reported or my reading)>". If nothing consequential happened, write "- None today." Do not list weight 0-2 items, process counts, query counts or what was searched. On Mondays add a last bullet "- New people and donors added: <names>".
 Put the same two sections, in the same format, in the final reply and in runs.summary (after "INCOMPLETE:" and "Deviations:" lines where those apply).
 BACKGROUND RULE (added Nov 2, 2026 after the reviewer's feedback): every CONSEQUENTIAL NEWS bullet must stand alone for a reader who has not followed the story. Name the parties and say what the case, act or dispute is ABOUT (what was asked for, by whom, against whom, why it matters), what happened to it before, and what happens next, in two to four plain sentences before the consequence. Never use a bare shorthand such as 'both ballot suits' or 'the Alaska suit' without saying what it is. Expand faction names on first use (say what the faction is). Do the same for entries in ERRORS AND INCOMPLETE ITEMS: say what each item is and why it matters, not only its label. Also write the same background into each stored item's context field for weight 3+ items, so the page is not terse either.
