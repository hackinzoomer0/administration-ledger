READER COPY — REQUIRED DAILY AND WEEKLY DATABASE WRITE STEP
Draft research first. Then do a deliberate second pass as a reader who has not followed the investigation or processing. This is a single analyst's separate critique, not an independent editor unless a genuinely separate reviewer was authorized and used. Do not ask the owner to supply these routine editorial judgments.

Use the runner-provided copy-plan command to generate reader-copy-plan.json. It inventories changed public prose with before/after text, locations and fingerprints. Read it by document or small groups, not as a giant tool dump. It covers maintained assessments, events, issues, factions, people, financial relationships, briefings and the parts of the latest wargame used by the site. Processing logs and historical model audits are separate records; do not copy them into public political explanations.

For EACH changed document, critique the final text:
1. Can a reader identify who is involved, their relevant job, what happened or might happen, and the decision at stake without reading earlier reports?
2. Does it explain the actual mechanism and consequence? Replace "agenda access" with the person's ability to decide which officials/proposals reach Trump; replace "national conversion" with recruiting voters, donors, staff and volunteers outside a home state. Name the actual actor, policy, organization or relationship when established.
3. Are vague references resolved? Explain "later planning ties" as the specific Project 2025 staffing role where that is the evidence. State what a business relationship is and why it matters; never substitute generic suspicion or infer misconduct from a payment alone.
4. Does each paragraph make a distinct point in ordinary sentences? Avoid compressed fragments, arrows standing in for explanations, strings of institutional nouns, undefined abbreviations and allusions to prior processing. A title must make sense on its own.
5. Are facts, reported claims, forecasts and genuine unknowns distinct? Explain what is unknown and why it affects the political outcome, rather than merely saying it was not verified in a snapshot. Preserve dates, qualifications, uncertainty, scope and source references. Do not invent context to make a sentence vivid. If detail cannot be established, explain the limitation plainly.
6. Is a technical explanation deliberately in a methods/full-record section with its purpose explained? Formulas and source records can be technical. Default assessments, candidate prospects, policy descriptions and power changes must explain the real world.

Repair the staged DATABASE fields themselves. Replacing UI labels or polishing the final chat report is insufficient. Re-read the corrected paragraph without the source material next to it. Preserve all analytical distinctions and numbers; editorial repair alone is not a new forecast, factual verification or reason to advance political research dates.

Regenerate copy-plan after changes. In the new run's reader_copy_review record:
{standard: "reader_copy_v1", mode: "single_analyst_second_pass", documents: {"COLLECTION/ID": {fingerprint: exact final fingerprint from the plan, reader_takeaway: what a reader should understand, context_check: what names/mechanisms/terms were explained or already clear, uncertainty_check: how facts/claims/forecasts/unknowns remain distinct, changes_made: [actual editorial corrections; empty only with a concrete reason in context_check]}}}.
Cover exactly the plan's documents, including an empty map if no public prose changed. Never copy fingerprints before completing the critique. Any later text edit makes the review stale: regenerate and recheck the affected document. Run the final preflight check. The validator verifies coverage and freshness, not whether subjective writing judgments are correct; do not describe it as automatic proof of readable prose.

Weekly, also inspect existing reader-facing copy beyond the latest edits, rotating among issue/faction pages and person/donor profiles. Repair examples of unexplained shorthand where existing evidence supports a clear explanation; record what was sampled and remaining problems in run notes. Keep a clear distinction between an editorial audit and new source verification. Daily work remains focused on changed copy and does not add a full wargame or require rechecking every historical record.
