At the end of every daily intake, explicitly review collections.assessments.current alongside factions and nominations. Write a concise current synthesis grounded in the SAME dated episodes, money relationships, actions and positions. Weekly reviews challenge it against later evidence. Do not generate rankings from event counts, graph centrality, donations or legacy scores.
Keep administration contests issue-specific. Distinguish formal authority, other leverage, announced change, exercised authority and achieved outcome. Each contest needs a short summary naming the actors and their leverage, assessment, authority, leverage, change, status, basis.actions, basis.positions, basis.money, watch conditions, event_ids and faction_ids. Use pending, contested, strengthening, weakening, unchanged or not_reviewed honestly. Financial exposure is not proof of donor direction. Preserve unresolved questions; no forced daily movement.
Keep exactly the authorized candidate summaries, linked by candidate_id, with path, coalition, advantage, vulnerability, positions, money, event_ids, source_ids, reviewed_at and a resolved person_id or null. Percentages remain ONLY in nominations; do not duplicate or recalibrate them in this projection. Keep nomination.snapshot_id linked to the latest substantive daily_review, weekly_review or faction_consistency_review snapshot. Imports, workflow migrations, UI audits and publication are not substantive forecast updates.
Update review.run_id to the new collections.runs entry, review.date to the run date, review.type to daily_review (or weekly_review), and review.scope to the ACTUAL coverage and gaps. research_as_of and each reviewed_at describe evidence coverage, not publication. Only advance last_substantive_change when a political conclusion materially changes. Carried findings must remain labeled and contradictions or stale summaries must be resolved or explicitly flagged. If blocked, preserve supported assessments, mark unreviewed scope, and do not imply new verification.
Cross-implications and explicit relationships need dated episode references. Use explicit faction IDs, scoped issues, a meaningful relationship kind and an explanation. Shared positions, documented coordination, money, access transitions and disputes are different relationships. Candidate forecasts are never independent evidence for faction conclusions.
Summarize changes or holds to the current assessment in the final daily message. Detailed news intake, verification failures and technical activity remain in the run record. The runner validates this assessment before publication or application.

Maintain collections.assessments.issues for EVERY tracked issue: current policy, disputes, named player_ids, structured faction positions/actions/goals, source episode references and research date. No placeholder for a missing synthesis is an acceptable daily result. Unknown policy details should be explained specifically, without omitting the known policy or inventing facts. Keep positions declarative and distinct from dated acts. Update the role-access scenarios when personnel changes; retain the explicit 0–5 rubric, assumptions, current versus conditional after-state and policy projections. Never describe these ordinal judgments as measured overall power or probabilities.

REALITY-FIRST SYNTHESIS — REQUIRED EVERY DAILY AND WEEKLY RUN
Reconstruct the overall assessment from the state of affairs established by the intake. Do not preserve the previous headline, its topic priorities or a fixed political narrative merely because it reads well. The post-midterm framing is an example of material synthesis, not a permanent template. Ask: what now dominates the administration's choices; who actually gained or lost authority, access, money or electoral leverage; what changed in policy versus personnel or rhetoric; what rivals and external institutions can do next; and what would demonstrate a change of course. Cover all tracked issues, not only the newest items. Search reactions and countermoves following major shocks. Reconcile the summary, issue policies, faction positions, people, power scenarios and candidate assessments against the same evidence.
Before finishing, compare every material development with the product output. Either reflect it in the appropriate assessment or explicitly explain why it does not change that assessment. Recheck dated assumptions, announced handovers, pending votes and expired deadlines: an anticipated event cannot stay prospective indefinitely without a documented verification gap. Keep unknowns visible and never infer an outcome just because its scheduled date passed.
An unchanged summary is acceptable only after substantive reassessment with reasons. A new review date is not evidence that an old conclusion remains valid. Do not force movement in power scores or nomination estimates. Keep actual authority separate from projected effects and update or retire scenarios when their assumptions resolve.
In the NEW collections.runs entry, record assessment_audit with:
- major_developments: list of {finding, event_ids, disposition: reflected|held|background, affected_assessments: list of product paths, reason}. Include the material developments actually identified, using valid episode IDs; if there are none, use an empty list and explain in summary_reason.
- issue_coverage: map of EVERY issue code to {status: reviewed|carried|unreviewed, reason}. Carried or unreviewed coverage must explain the gap and its effect on the current assessment; it cannot be presented as fresh verification.
- summary_changed: boolean comparing the actual administration headline/summary with the previous version; summary_reason: why this is now the right overall interpretation or why it holds.
- dated_assumptions: list of {assumption, date, status: verified|pending|unreviewed, reason}. Include relevant passed or imminent triggers; identify verification failures rather than silently retaining outdated future tense.
- challenges: list of {alternative, finding, consequence}. Weekly must test at least one plausible competing interpretation, overlooked beneficiary, rival response or disconfirming item and explain the resulting correction or reason to retain the assessment.
Update the current review reference even when conclusions hold. The final message must explain the resulting overall assessment and any major carried/unreviewed limitations, not simply report how many records changed.

ISSUE DETAIL QUALITY
Maintain each issue’s actors array, covering player_ids exactly once: person_id, role, faction_id (or null), position, goal, financial_relationships and basis_note. Use the actor’s ISSUE-SPECIFIC position and incentive, not a general biography or motives from another issue. Clearly distinguish a documented individual position, a faction stance and a role-based analytical inference. Do not silently drop external oversight actors when individual-position detail is incomplete; explain the precise limitation. Financial relationships require name, relationship (recipient, period, funding versus investment/client/exposure) and issue relevance. Never populate them from all matching donors or arbitrary first matches. A shared connection is not political backing, and financial exposure is not proof of influence. Preserve dated claims and uncertainty. Keep the structured candidate base, historical financial relationships, consistency timeline and source evidence readable and distinct; do not substitute generic field dumps for a profile.

Maintain nomination.scenario_labels = {wargame_ref, scenarios: {SCENARIO_ID: {title, summary}}} for the latest full wargame, including during daily monitoring. Titles must name concrete events and actors so a reader can distinguish the cases without opening them; summaries explain what happens and who reacts. Preserve the original scenario meaning and numerical inputs. Avoid abstract shorthand such as "costs persist; leadership retains a coalition". Labels are presentation copy, not a new forecast or new political evidence; do not advance political review dates for copy changes alone.


POLITICAL EXPLANATIONS VERSUS MODEL AUDITS
The maintained nomination candidate path/advantage/vulnerability/coalition fields, public forecast_rationale, and reader-facing wargame explanations must describe real-world prospects: actions, policy positions, voter perceptions, rivals, donors, entry, organization and consequences. Never put instructions to the forecaster, corrections to inherited numerical ceilings/floors, normalization, schema references or conversations about adjusting the algorithm into those fields. "Remove the inherited 1–3 ceiling" explains an audit decision; it does not explain a candidate's prospects. Explain instead what could make voters support the candidate and what could stop them.
Record model corrections, competing numerical allocations and computational reasoning in the designated comparison/weighting audit fields and run log. Do not copy those paragraphs into the political summaries. Keep voter_response, candidate_response, organization_coalition and other causal narrative fields about projected people and events. Before completion, review the exact nomination text a reader will see and reject any paragraph that speaks to the reviewer about scoring rather than explaining the race. Preserve forecast history and numbers when fixing presentation copy; use the maintained current-assessment layer for editorial corrections.

Reader-facing database text must pass reader-copy-review before publication. Explain names, roles, decisions, mechanisms and consequences in ordinary sentences; do not assume readers know prior reports or internal shorthand. The required separate editorial pass repairs the stored fields and records final-text fingerprints, context and uncertainty checks in the run. Technical material belongs in explicitly identified methods/full-record sections.

Candidate coalition explanations must identify actual supporters versus potential constituencies, why they support the candidate, whose continued alignment matters and what they can change through voting, organizing, money, attention or withholding support. Explain the strongest consequential finding in ordinary language using existing coalition/path/advantage/vulnerability fields; do not copy base_assessment/base_effects schema or internal scoring instructions into the site. An influencer endorsement is not automatic voter transfer, and a defection threat is not observed defection. Reflect newly sourced developments daily while carrying the dated full-weekly odds honestly.

Apply research-questions: explicitly weigh explanations, separate future likelihood/impact, and conclude how the evidence changes the assessment. Do not give negligible alternatives artificial balance or duplicate the causal narrative.
