# Personnel impeachment and conviction review

Maintain collections.impeachments.all in the Administration/personnel feature using the existing shared daily/weekly intake. No separate timer or extra research chat. Run targeted retrieval on this document; do not infer no risk from missing faction membership, fewer headlines, departure, or an unestimated watchlist entry. Discover cases across current and former officials, not a fixed set of names supplied in conversation. Roster expansion here is permitted with a documented case, source, eligibility assessment and a new forecast/watchlist row; nomination roster/methodology authorization remains unchanged.

DAILY: verify actual scheduled House/Senate dispositions and new filings, evidence, personnel changes, defenses, investigative findings, coalition commitments and political reactions. Append a review even when all odds hold; cover every current case, describe the actual scope/gaps and link the same new daily run. Reassess impeachment odds only when material evidence or corrected assumptions warrant it; no automatic extra full nomination review or nominee odds change. WEEKLY: compare all cases under the same evidence, agenda, eligibility, timing and Senate-coalition questions; challenge omitted cases, unequal coverage, owner/analyst framing, selection effects and stale estimates. Retain, retire with reason or replace dated tests without erasing original claims/deadlines.

Keep these distinct: House adoption of at least one article; Senate conviction conditional on impeachment within the window; their joint probability by the same deadline; removal/resignation; criminal proceedings. Percentages across people are not shares and need not sum to100. joint_pct = round(house_pct * conviction_given_impeachment_pct /100,2). Store all three null for unestimated cases, never zero as a coverage penalty. Judgment ranges are sensitivity ranges, not statistical intervals. No allegation-count or faction score formula. Identify evidence supporting a specific personal act and the best surviving defense, plus actual and projected political choices. Explain why the assigned probability is preferable to a plausible alternative before holding or changing it. Check civil-officer eligibility and timing of alleged conduct; do not assume all White House employees are impeachable. Departure does not automatically bar proceedings: examine accountability/disqualification incentives and contested jurisdiction separately.

SHARED MOMENTUM: outcomes are correlated. A first Senate conviction can lower the perceived cost of party defection, demonstrate feasibility, encourage House action and witness cooperation, and create a reusable coalition even for entirely unrelated allegations. Conversely the case may be treated as exceptional, exhaust appetite or create backlash. Weigh these mechanisms instead of mechanically applying a positive uplift. Investigate the coalition, president's support/abandonment, public and party response, consequences for supporting senators, House agenda, witness behavior and whether the coalition carries to a second case. A convicted official's case is not evidence of anyone else's misconduct.

Record verified milestones in append-only proceedings, with sources, actual date, precise kind and case_id. A motion to table, referral or vote to proceed is not House impeachment; House impeachment is not Senate conviction. Correct an erroneous milestone with a later row whose supersedes references the original. Momentum observations must link the relevant proceedings, source/event evidence, actual political response, coalition, scope and countercase. A conviction observation needs a real senate_conviction proceeding, not a speculative headline. No observed conviction state without that evidence. Initial unverified means none was verified within reviewed coverage; it does not assert an exhaustive historical census.

For every review, set context state, assessment and observation_ids, then address normalization, exception and backlash pathways. After a new momentum observation, reassess EVERY case: record up/down/hold/mixed/unquantified, the relevant observation_ids and a concrete causal reason. Distinguish related-evidence spillover from unrelated-case momentum. No affected case may silently inherit unchanged odds: explain a hold if it remains unchanged. If numerical effect is not yet supportable, say unquantified and flag possible staleness rather than fabricate a multiplier. Distinguish outcome evidence from effects merely inferred before measurable reactions; do not require polling to make a grounded qualitative inference.

Append immutable history with unique id, timezone-aware as_of, deadline, same run_id as current-assessment.review, type daily/weekly, summary, coverage, shared context and forecasts for ALL cases. Each forecast includes current reason/confidence, three probabilities and joint_range_pct, momentum effect, and a historical basis snapshot containing allegations, defense, office, eligibility, watch, source_ids and event_ids. Preserve older snapshots and original sources; add a new source record or correction instead of altering evidence attached to an earlier forecast. Keep existing cases as watchlist/closed cases rather than deleting them. For concluded cases record the outcome and explain whether new forecasts concern future articles; never pretend the original forecast was made after the result. Resolve due tests as met/not_met/unresolved/retired with substantive explanations and evidence, not perpetual pending.

Maintain traceable political consequences in the relevant existing administration/person/faction/issue assessments where supported; distinguish momentum from personal guilt. Nomination implications enter the existing daily monitor or weekly game at their authorized cadence, with no automatic odds changes. Read and execute reader-copy-review for changed personnel prose. Final scheduled report must state material case/probability changes or an explicit hold, actual proceedings, momentum developments and affected cases, outstanding tests and coverage gaps. Label subjective odds and the deadline; never present them as measured conviction support.

For a verified House adoption or Senate conviction, record outcomes.house/outcomes.conviction as occurred and link the actual case-specific proceeding_ids in the new forecast. Resolve the corresponding probability to100 (House first, both for conviction) and render it as an occurred outcome, not a fresh prediction. Keep pending otherwise; acquittal does not automatically exclude a later conviction on another article within the same window. Preserve earlier forecasts for scoring. Record every new shared momentum observation in each case's response/hold assessment, even if its significance is negligible. Context must include the new observations so they cannot silently disappear.
