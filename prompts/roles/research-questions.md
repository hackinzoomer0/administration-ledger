RESEARCH PRIORITIES AND EXPLICIT WEIGHT OF EVIDENCE
Execute within the existing daily/weekly review using one shared intake. Read research-plan.json first. Select 1–5 consequential unanswered questions before searching: what answer could change the current assessment, candidate comparison or near-term decision watch? Prioritize consequence, uncertainty, urgency, undercoverage and an obtainable discriminating observation, not equal source counts or the most elaborate prose. Include one discovery question outside the inherited actor/issue frame. Reserve a catch-up task for an overdue consequential gap; failed access and unattempted work are different.

Weigh explanations rather than merely listing alternatives. For each priority state the best current answer and any serious competing explanation with weight dominant, credible, limited, negligible or contradicted, reasons and a discriminating observation. Do not manufacture an alternative for balance; a well-established answer may stand alone. A negligible/contradicted explanation has no material influence unless evidence changes. Coexisting causes are not mutually exclusive shares: explain their joint contribution without forcing them to sum to 100. Qualitative weights are the default; use justified numerical ranges only where they resolve a consequential disagreement, identifying whether alternatives are exclusive/exhaustive. Do not multiply these judgments into candidate odds or add a second scoring formula.

For a future development, record its likelihood (low/moderate/high/unknown), impact (low/moderate/high), why, and what it would change. An unlikely high-impact event can merit monitoring; it is not a likely explanation of present facts. Research priority is the usefulness of obtaining an answer, not another probability. Conclude which explanation carries the weight and why, which countercase survives at what weight, and what observation could materially change that balance. Do not give unsupported possibilities equal treatment.

Ask from decisions and outcomes inward as well as known actors outward. Who can initiate, authorize, fund, implement, delay, review or reverse the particular decision? Who is missing from the faction map but holds a chokepoint? What does the target/counterparty want, what are its alternatives, and how can it adapt or spoil the policy? Trace the weakest consequential link from announcement through resources, implementation, compliance, outcome and adaptation. Distinguish endorsement influence from voters, state rules, delegates, entry and organization. No automatic nominee additions.

Compare sincere ideological/policy/institutional motives with career, money and patron explanations when plausible. A beneficiary is not necessarily an influencer, a financial tie is not proof of direction, and a policy cost can be evidence of sincere priority. Look for costly choices and contrary cases. Do not presume either hidden corruption or pure stated motives. Before using holders/policy/motives, reconcile them against maintained current assessments and dated evidence; legacy summaries are historical context, never current authority by default. Update supported maintained summaries; preserve historical forecasts.

Keep a private receipt for each actual priority-related query/source attempt using the provided helper. Record kind, value (URL/query), window, purpose, status (read/headline_only/blocked/not_found), result, reporting_origin, and an optional short lawful excerpt. IDs are returned by the helper. Check lookup before re-querying; inspect age and refresh mutable indexes. Cache/excerpts do not bypass blocked access or independently corroborate a claim. Shared syndication has one reporting origin. Use claim-level source/event IDs when updating consequential active claims; publication/event/access dates and evidence limits matter. An original report, attributed private report and forecast are different evidence.

Absence claims require a search that could reasonably detect the evidence: what should be public if true, where/when did we look, what was inaccessible? Unknown, not attempted, attempted but inaccessible, adequately searched/no public evidence, corroborated and contradicted are distinct dispositions. For contradictions compare exact proposition, event/publication dates, original source and corrections; avoid silently overwriting the older record.

Record this run's research_review = {standard: "question_review_v1", priorities: [{id, question, importance, affected_assessments: [paths], current_answer, explanations: [{explanation, weight, reason, discriminating_observation}], change_triggers: [{development, likelihood, impact, reason, assessment_effect}], status: answered|partial|blocked|deferred, outcome, next_action, next_check: YYYY-MM-DD, source_receipt_ids: [returned IDs]}], prior_dispositions: {OLD_UNANSWERED_ID: reason}, coverage_debt: {ISSUE: {last_verified: YYYY-MM-DD|null, next_action, next_check: YYYY-MM-DD, reason, escalation: reason if unknown or at least seven days stale}}, discovery_question, discovery_disposition, premise_reconciliation}. If no meaningful change trigger exists, use an empty list and explain no_change_trigger_reason. No invented receipt for unattempted work. Answered is a question disposition, not proof of certainty. All carried unanswered IDs need continuation or disposition. last_verified advances only for genuinely reviewed issue coverage, using the dated baseline in research-plan.json; a new timestamp or stored-event screen is not fresh external verification.

Weekly also record concise omitted_mechanism, test_discrimination, implementation_counterparty, motive_alternatives, prediction_outcomes and candidate_share_sensitivity. Challenge one future path outside the current dominant shock and one overlap, explaining retain/revise decisions without quota-driven new scenarios. Ask whether prediction outcomes distinguish causes rather than merely fit them. Compound claims need separate dispositions. Select tests from consequential differences between forecasts, not equal candidate quotas; add interim observations without rewriting original terminal tests/deadlines. Historical design errors receive a replacement ID/reason, not a retroactive pass. Audit open patterns and unmatched/unmodeled leads with incorporate/defer/retire decisions and next observations. Explain shortfalls in recall_shortfall when fewer than 15 actual dated samples/two discovery sources were checked. Partial work may publish useful findings, but debt must remain visible.

Use scripts/wargame.py output for candidate-share as well as environment-weight stresses and prediction outcome inventory. Stresses are hypothetical input perturbations, not confidence intervals or empirical calibration. Do not assign numerical calibration scores to qualitative predictions. Compare prior claims with actual outcomes and explain which premise changed. Where a meaningful public baseline exists, record the date, comparable target and discrepancy; do not conflate poll preference with eventual nomination probability.

Reuse established support/base and biography evidence. Write each candidate/scenario causal explanation once; comparison, appeal and support audits should reference the consequential chain rather than duplicate it. Do not add a daily full matrix, extra run, independent reviewer claim, new odds formula or automatic shock rerun. Record actual scope and reasons for unequal coverage. Reader-copy-review remains required after the substantive work.

Include research_review.source_receipts as the nonprivate evidence projection of used receipts: id, kind, value, window, purpose, status, result and reporting_origin. Exclude mailbox, chat, account, tool-response and runtime metadata. This supports CI traceability; it is not independent proof of source truth. The stage helper remains authoritative for attempts made this run.

Apply the same explicit plausibility judgment to every material countercase outside the priority register: state its weight and why in the existing resolution/rebuttal field. Do not build another all-candidate matrix or give irrelevant alternatives space. New weekly game judgments and candidate debates include countercase_weight using the same five labels; the existing resolution/verdict explains its actual influence. Scenario weights already express future-environment likelihood, not evidence strength, and must not be counted twice. Appeal, base and comparison countercases likewise explain why they materially affect the causal path or carry negligible weight.

For overdue/unknown coverage, include attempt_receipt_ids in the relevant coverage_debt entry and reserve at least one actual catch-up attempt per run. If the run could not attempt any intake, explain catch_up_shortfall and retain incomplete status. Set next_check to today or a future date; never leave an already-past follow-up hidden beneath a new review date.
