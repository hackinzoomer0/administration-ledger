DATABASE SHAPE
- `events/<ISSUE>`: one document per issue code IRN, FED, SUC, AI, MID, TAR, IMM, VEN, EPS, RUS, each {issue, items:[...]}. Item: {id, date "YYYY-MM-DD" (or "YYYY-MM" with precision "month"), precision, issues:[codes], kind "shift"|"act"|"chron", actor, text, context, source, tier, weight 0-5, rationale, origin, added, by:[faction ids], fx:{faction id: 1|-1}, fxnote, mergedFrom?:[{id, kind, weight, text, context, source, tier, added}]}. One item per occurrence across ALL issue documents: an event that touches several issues is stored once, in its main issue's document, with every code in `issues` (the page counts influence by `issues`, not by document). `mergedFrom` lists folded duplicates; keep it and never delete it. An item that fits no issue well is stored in the closest issue's document with the plausible issue codes and a context line saying the link is a hypothesis.
- `runs/<YYYY-MM-DD>`: {added:number, summary:string, coverage:{sourcesRead:[...], queries:number, queriesPerIssue:{CODE:N}, perIssue:{CODE:"found N"|"searched, nothing found"|"not searched"}, unplaced:[short sentences], blocked:[sources that could not be fetched], gaps:[{gap, routes:[{route, tried, whyItFailed}]}], watched:[names checked in the watch pass], queue:{captured:N, triaged:N}, lookbackDays:N, thinDays:[dates with fewer than two independent sources], deviations:[sentences; empty array if none], propagation:{itemId:[docs changed] or ["none: reason"]}, fxStats:{scored:N, empty:N, emptyWithReason:N}, methodAudit:{stage1:{sourcesEnumerated:[...], headlinesTriaged:N, kept:N, recallSample:{sampled:N, missingFromLedgerOrTriage:N, items:[...]}}, stage2:{itemsKept:N}, foundOnlyByStage1:[item ids or short names], foundOnlyByStage2:[item ids or short names], missedByBoth:[items found later, in the watch pass, in the loop or from the reviewer], rootCause:string, proposedFix:string}, checklist:{key:"done"|"partial: reason"|"skipped: reason"}}}.
- `changes/<YYYY-MM-DD>`: {items:[short plain sentences saying which report text you revised and why]}. The page shows these under "What changed in this report".
- `factions/<id>` (ids: operation, maximalists, treasury, deal-capital, tech-right, vance, hawks, pentagon, populist, dissenters, party): {name, short, wants, why, did, weak, overview:[paras], members:[rows], donors:[rows], legal:[rows], legalnote, positions:{ISSUE:[{title, paras}]}, gained, lost, win, blunder, cause, edges}. members rows [Name, Role, Backs the faction on, On-side 1-10, Driver]; donors rows [Donor or patron, How much, Who in the faction, Topics, What the actors gain]. The `legal` rows are superseded by meta/legal; leave them.
- `issues/<code>`: {code, name, decision, heldBy, pending}.
- `meta/channels`: {asof, note, channels:{IRN,FED,SUC,AI,MID,TAR,IMM,VEN,EPS,RUS,WORLD: string listing causal channels}}. A starting aid for Stage 2, NOT a filter and NOT used by Stage 1: an item that matches no listed channel is still kept if the critical read finds any effect. Extend it (with the date) whenever you meet a new channel.
- `meta/world`: {asof, note, items:[{id, date, issues:[codes], status "open"|"settled", text (what happened, under 220 chars), implication (the NET EFFECT sentence from step 3(h), labeled "(my reading)", under 220 chars), caveat (what is unsettled or unverified, under 220 chars), source}]}. Shown on the Home cards and the Issues page (up to two open items per issue, newest first). See step 12.
- `meta/motives`: {asof, note, types:[...], issues:{ISSUE:{decision, alternative, factions:{factionId:{type, motive, gain, diff, tier, basis}}}}}. `type` is one of power, money, access, position, survival, legal cover, audience, ideology, state interest. `motive` = what the faction is after for itself (under 260 characters). `gain` = what it gets from the current decision. `diff` = integer -2 to 2: how much better (positive) or worse (negative) off the faction is under the current decision than under the alternative. `tier` = evidence tier. `basis` = the dated fact the reading rests on. Positive diffs add weight to the faction's influence percent on that issue, so keep them honest. Populated so far: IRN, MID and FED; see step 13.
- `meta/patterns`: {asof, note, patterns:[{id, title, summary, issues:[codes], factions:[ids], evidence:[event ids], test, disproof, status "open"|"supported"|"retired", label "my reading", firstSeen, lastChecked}]}. See step 11. If you fold an event into another, replace its id in `evidence`.
- `meta/policy`: {asof, IRN, FED, SUC, AI, MID, TAR, IMM, VEN, EPS, RUS}: ONE plain sentence per issue (under 200 characters) stating what the administration's policy is right now.
- `meta/stances`: {asof, note, stances:{ISSUE:{factionId:{what, why, tag}}}}. For each faction that matters on an issue: `what`, `why` (labeled documented fit / reported / my reading; never say money caused a decision unless a source documents it), `tag`. Under 260 characters each.
- `meta/holders`: {asof, note, ISSUE:{decider:string, tags:{factionId: short role label}}}. Labels with decid/hold/sets/preemption/military count full, others half.
- `meta/stakes`: {asof, note, stakes:{factionId:[[Name or names as in the faction's members table, stake text with lines starting "Lastname: ..."]]}}. Documented money, family and business stakes from the report, shown as "Stakes and ties" on person pages (matched by last name) and on faction rosters. Update a row only when a dated, documented fact changes a stake; for people added later, put stakes in their `pattern` instead.
- `meta/legal`: {asof, note, levels, entries:[{id, names, subjects:[people/all ids], factions:[ids], conduct, theory, level "court"|"record"|"reported"|"anon"|"unproven", levelNote, confirmed, unconfirmed, exposure "High"|"Moderate"|"Low"|"Not established", exposureWhy, probers, escalates, asof}]}. Feeds the Legal exposure sections on faction and person pages. See step 17.
- `meta/queue`: {asof, items:[{id, firstSeen, kind "person"|"donor"|"departure"|"role-change"|"ownership"|"actor", name, what (one line), source, status "new"|"added"|"dropped", note}]}. The capture queue; see step 16. Kind "actor" = an unmodeled actor (see step 3(a)).
- `meta/audit`: {asof, weekOf, contradicted:[{belief, where, event ids, proposed edit}], unmodeled:[{actor, events, proposed profile}], unmatched:[{event ids, what stored belief is missing}], applied:[sentences]}. Written by the Monday model audit (step 18).
- `report/<key>` (bottom, method, overview, money, dynamics, legal, limits): each {key, title, asof, paras:[...], plus extras: overview.relnotes/decomp, dynamics.watch (rows [Date, Event, Factions tested, Hypothesis]) /watchnote/disnote, limits.terms}.
- `meta/dynamics`: {edges: table rows (first row header) [Edge, Type Feud|Alliance|Mixed, Issues, Evidence, Documented link?, Where it would split], straddlers: table rows (first row header) [Person, Home faction, Also acts for, Where they break, Why they do it], terms}. `meta/pipes`: {cols, rows:[{name, detail, cells:{faction id:"d"|"r"}}], note}.
- `people/all`: {list:[person], note}. ONE ENTRY PER PERSON: never combine several people in one entry (a group named in a report, such as a state delegation or "Thiel, Andreessen", is split into individual entries). person = {id (slug of name), name (first and last name, never a surname alone), role (the individual's own office, for example "Senator (R-Kansas)", never a group label), home (faction id or null), also:[faction ids], rosters:[{f, onSide, backs, driver}], positions:{ISSUE:{label, view, status, role}}, straddle:{breaks, evidence} (optional), donors:[donor ids], added ("YYYY-MM-DD", set only on NEW entries), mtype, read, pattern, drive, why, flip, mtier, motive}. CHARACTER fields follow step 14: `mtype` = archetype (for example chameleon, operator, dependent, power-builder, profiteer, careerist, audience-builder, survivor, free agent, enforcer, performer, insurgent building an exit, opportunist, courtier, hedging owner-operator, power-seeker, or "thin record"; never "true believer" or "loyalist"); `read` = ONE decisive sentence or two, no competing readings; `pattern` = array of dated cross-issue facts that show it (each under 220 chars); `drive` = the one core thing they want; `why` = {ISSUE code: one sentence on their motive on that issue, plain words}; `flip` = what would change the read; `mtier` = evidence tier of the underlying facts; `motive` = copy of `drive`. Keep `donors` and donors/all `people` consistent in both directions.
- `donors/all`: {list:[donor], note}. ONE ENTRY PER PERSON OR ORGANIZATION: never combine names ("Adelson, Singer" is two entries; "Nvidia and Huang" is two); an unnamed class ("oil and gas donors", "MAGA Inc. donors") is not an entry; a giving vehicle (a super PAC, fund or company) is its own entry. donor = {id, name, identity, amounts:[strings about this donor only], issues:[codes], supports:[codes of the issues the donor pushes], factions:[ids], views:[], beneficiaries:[], evidence:[], who:[], gain, topics:[], people:[person ids], tier, contractor? (true for government contractors), mtype, motive, mtier, mergedFrom:[old ids]?, added (set only on NEW entries)}. `identity` = who the donor actually is, in one plain line a newcomer can use (for example "Larry Ellison, Oracle co-founder and chair"). Before adding, check for an existing entry under another spelling or as a vehicle of the same person, and merge instead. Keep each doc under 256 KiB; fields under 220 chars (motive under 260).
- The page computes each faction's influence percent and 90-day trend per issue from events (by/fx tags, recency decay), meta/holders and the positive diffs in meta/motives, so tag events carefully and never store the same occurrence twice.
- Never write issue codes (IRN, FED, etc.) into prose fields shown to the reviewer, including titles and table cells: write the issue name in full (Iran, the Fed, succession, AI policy, midterms, tariffs, immigration, Venezuela, the Epstein files, Russia). Codes are allowed only as keys and in `issues`/`supports` arrays.


Optional review fields are meta/stances.stance_audit and coverage, meta/holders.decision_audit, event_reconciliation and outcome_review, meta/dynamics.reviewed_edges, donor source_check, and legal caseParticipants (display names). Keep legal subjects as profile IDs. Preserve legacy fields consumed by existing readers.
