# Reader-facing content audit

The site should explain who can change administration policy and how Republicans might choose their next nominee. A reader should not have to reconstruct the research process to understand either assessment.

This editorial review uses the existing evidence. It does not constitute a fresh news intake, change any odds, verify every archived allegation or advance political research dates.

## Daily and weekly database-writing contract

Both scheduled prompts now require the `reader-copy-review` role after drafting research. The runner generates an inventory of changed public prose, including deletions, with before/after text and a fingerprint per document. This includes the site-facing portions of the latest full wargame, including when reached through a daily snapshot.

The writer must take a separate second pass, repair the database text itself and record each document’s reader takeaway, context check, uncertainty check and corrections in the new run. This is explicitly a single analyst’s second pass, not an independent editor. A later edit invalidates the saved fingerprint. Publication and application fail if coverage or fingerprints are missing or stale. The final chat report cannot substitute for corrections to database fields.

The review asks whether a reader can identify the actor, relevant role, action or choice, mechanism and consequence. It requires ordinary sentences, explained terms and clear distinctions between fact, allegation, forecast and unknown. It forbids inventing details to make prose vivid. Calculation and historical source records can remain technical when deliberately presented as such.

The gate establishes coverage and freshness. It cannot certify that subjective editorial judgments are good. Weekly reviewers must also sample existing issue/faction and person/donor records, report their scope and repair supported examples of shorthand. Daily reviews stay focused on changed text and do not add another full wargame.

## Remaining scope

The audit traced the main assessment, power, issue, faction, person, financial-relationship and nomination presentation paths. It did not individually rewrite or independently source-check all 202 people and 160 financial profiles, nor every historical event and scenario paragraph. Older supporting records still need the rotating weekly review. The historical raw record stays available, with its original dates and qualifications; the durable protection is review of future database writes, supplemented by that ongoing backlog review.
