The main corrections and qualifications:

- Vance: the working assessment now includes the ledger’s concrete finance, fundraising and organizational channels. It recognizes the strategic benefit of his public post-election distinction without claiming new polling gains. Carlson/Kent audiences remain contestable; Don Jr.’s agreement with a message is not an endorsement by every associated actor. The technology network also has policy tensions, especially on skilled immigration.
- Rubio: the hawkish label does not explain every action. His staffing platform and relationship with Trump matter alongside donors. The ledger distinguishes donors preferring him from their eventual support if another contender becomes the viable nominee. Public deferral and his reported denial of campaign-related departure remain entry constraints.
- DeSantis: distance from federal decision-making can help, but does not itself transfer the Party Machine, Treasury, hawk donors or disaffected national conservatives to him. The wargame’s 1.30-point gap behind Rubio is an assumption-sensitive judgment. This is the clearest gap between a plausible scenario and a demonstrated coalition.
- Cruz: historical donors are not present campaign commitments. Rubio and Cruz cannot each receive the full value of the same faction’s resources as though those resources were exclusive. The distinction between hawkish policy, tariff skepticism and nomination organization is retained.
- McEntee: corrected the overly empty immigration treatment. His nationalist staffing record supplies substantive evidence of policy direction. His Afghanistan/Somalia withdrawal work is supported by the committee record, and Heritage documents a personnel recruitment role. These support comparisons with restraint and executive-control goals; they establish neither present endorsements nor detailed current proposals. Thiel’s business investment stays separate from political backing. The older ledger’s lack of a McEntee profile is a research gap, not contrary evidence.

The comparison also corrected the faction side. Dated succession addenda now recognize Vance’s post-election positioning and the announced Miller/Wiles transition. The Operation’s older description of Wiles as White House gatekeeper cannot simply be carried forward. Access, campaign money and future endorsements are separate channels. The dissolved populist faction is treated as archival; it is not a unified constituency available for allocation to a candidate. No faction scores or public event rows changed.
