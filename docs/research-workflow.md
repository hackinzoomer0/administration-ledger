# Research priorities and remediation contract

Daily and weekly reviews execute `research-questions`. This is an operating improvement within the authorized forecast method: no new multipliers, roster additions or daily full forecast.

## Explicit weight of evidence

Explanations receive dominant, credible, limited, negligible or contradicted plausibility judgments with reasons. Only serious unresolved explanations receive material analytical attention. Do not invent false alternatives or give them equal weight. Joint causes are not forced to sum to 100. Numerical ranges are optional when useful and defensible; no unsupported precision is required.

Future developments receive separate likelihood and impact judgments. Research priorities depend on the consequence and usefulness of resolving a question. These three judgments are distinct and are never multiplied into nomination shares.

## Beliefs, reputation and political consequences

Factual verification and political effect are separate judgments. A disputed allegation can damage credibility, legitimacy or perceived competence; an accurate finding can have little immediate effect if relevant audiences do not encounter or accept it. Public beliefs can dominate electoral and reputational consequences, while authority and implementation retain separate evidence requirements.

For consequential allegations, insider defections and prominent interviews, trace the underlying claim and its verification status; the dated speaker/amplifiers and relevant audience; observed reception; supported inference about present vulnerability; and possible later behavior. Exposure is not acceptance, and self-selected reactions are not representative opinion. A direct episode, transcript, extracts and reporting have different coverage; identify what was actually reviewed. Search reception and counter-moves alongside factual corroboration, recording actual attempts and unattempted gaps.

Do not make polling, legal adjudication, completed policy changes or measured defections prerequisites for recognizing a well-grounded vulnerability. Uncertain reception limits confidence and magnitude. Conversely, do not assert mass persuasion or automatic support transfers from the existence of an attack. Use existing event/assessment fields and research-review explanations; explicitly weigh serious competing mechanisms and identify a discriminating observation.

Claims about administrative corruption or obstruction also test who can choose and supervise personnel. Trace responsibility through officials, their faction and the appointing leader; an accuser shielding the leader does not establish that audiences absolve the leader. Examine documented staffing capability and prior personnel decisions across rivals rather than automatically benefiting an outsider or personnel specialist.

Daily monitoring updates supported political summaries and queues weekly questions while holding odds. Weekly review incorporates these mechanisms once within the existing scenario mixture and common candidate standards. No new multiplier, roster change, automatic score or historical forecast rewrite is introduced. A held authority score or daily estimate must not be presented as evidence of unchanged credibility. Structural validation cannot establish audience beliefs or the quality of this inference.

## Small question register

Start with `research-plan.json` and choose one to five consequential questions. Shared questions link to affected assessments rather than being copied for every candidate. Attempted work has actual private intake receipts and an explicitly projected public evidence record. Deferred work remains labeled. Unanswered IDs must continue or have a disposition. Seven-day/unknown issue gaps require escalation, not an invented fresh verification date. These checks establish accountability, not completeness or political truth.

Source/query receipts normalize repeat lookups and record reporting origin, window, purpose, access result and optional short excerpt/hash. Mutable indexes must be refreshed; a cached blocked result is not proof that access remains blocked. Syndication is not independent corroboration. Receipts never contain mailbox identifiers or daemon/tool-response metadata. A receipt records the analyst's actual attempt; it is not a trusted content-fetch or independent verification service.

## Targeted retrieval

The generated prompt provides an exact command for `research_tools.py STAGE`:

- `get COLLECTION ID --path FIELD/PATH` retrieves one record or field.
- `find TEXT --limit N` finds relevant document IDs without dumping records.
- `lookup KIND VALUE WINDOW` reads a prior private receipt and its age.
- `receipt FILE.json` records an actual query/source attempt and returns its stable ID.
- `slice CANDIDATES.json` builds compact belief/duplicate slices from the staged ledger.

Current authority, policy and actor goals come from maintained assessments. Older meta summaries are preserved as historical context. The slicer's recent-event window limits output volume, not the age of evidence the reviewer may retrieve.

## Weekly diagnosis

Challenge omitted mechanisms and scenario overlap, implementation/counterparty responses, sincere versus instrumental motives, and whether tests distinguish rival explanations. Preserve original claims/deadlines; flawed tests get replacement IDs and reasons. Use the outcome inventory and within-scenario share stresses alongside environment-weight stresses. Neither stress is a confidence interval. Qualitative predictions do not support numerical Brier scores; empirical calibration remains unestablished.

The existing 40-path projection is retained, but explain each causal chain once and reference it from comparison, appeal and support audits. The latest full historical game does not retroactively satisfy requirements added afterward. New findings and reasoned model corrections can both change a weekly judgment; unrelated access gaps do not freeze it.

## Privacy and validation

Tracked memory contains impersonal current policy. Private operational/conversational history lives in ignored storage; prior public Git commits are not erased by current cleanup. Publication requires configured private identifiers locally or via the CI secret. Both publication paths scan tracked files; deployment also scans built assets. Checks reject identifying paths, configured owner terms, unapproved contacts, credentials and private mailbox/runtime values. Code/test patterns have scoped non-content exemptions; owner terms and credentials remain checked. Public political names must receive explicit reviewed exceptions, never a global exemption for a common first name.

One event/profile validator serves integrity checks, scheduled preflight and CI. Legacy exceptions cover only the content-hashed unchanged presentation violations listed in `legacy_event_exceptions.json`, not dates, faction IDs or future edits. Legacy scheduled-run IDs are explicitly listed; new scheduled runs require the question-review contract. New scheduled contract validation includes public evidence-receipt projections so direct pushes cannot omit the record.

## Browser and maintenance

Current nomination projection preserves historical probability points and the latest full game; older full games and review records load through the Research archive. Unused inbox/meta collections are excluded from browser imports. Rich current evidence still creates a substantial shared payload; the remaining build-size warning is visible rather than suppressed. Candidate policy rendering and qualitative backend review contracts have separate modules.

## Validation limits

Structural checks cannot establish calibration, good judgment or unbiased discovery. Privacy scanning cannot prove absence of all personally identifying inference. This change implements retrieval, prioritization, audit and recovery mechanisms; it does not claim to have completed outstanding political research or reverified the full historical ledger.
