# Current project policy

Canonical research lives in database/, prompts in prompts/, React in src/ and review tools in scripts/. Historical source snapshots remain in Git; personal conversational and operational memory belongs in ignored private storage.

The product leads with current administration power assessments and conditional 2028 nomination judgments. Keep evidence, inference, unknowns, announcements, implementation and outcomes distinct. Use decision-specific authority, leverage, incentives and opposition rather than shared slogans or activity scores.

Eight authorized nominees: JD Vance, Marco Rubio, Ron DeSantis, Ted Cruz, John McEntee, Rand Paul, Brian Kemp and Thomas Massie. No automatic roster or forecast-formula changes. Full predictive review is weekly; daily shares hold while material findings, due observations, stale-forecast warnings and maintained assessments update through shared intake.

Prioritize consequential research questions, explicitly weigh explanations, and distinguish future likelihood from impact and research priority. Negligible or contradicted alternatives receive no artificial balance. Infer cautiously before measurements exist; missing coverage is not proof of absence or an automatic numerical penalty. Preserve original forecasts/tests and label retrospective method changes.

Publish authorized research only after privacy checks, appropriate validation/CI and live revision/database-hash verification. Respect .runtime/review.lock. Keep identifiers, personal contacts, credentials, mailbox IDs and runtime metadata out of public records. Delivery failure must not rerun already applied research.

Use established Material UI, React Flow and Recharts. Public explanations describe political actors/actions/consequences in plain language; audit/schema and numerical adjudication belong in research methods. Current-assessment copy must match the latest substantive evidence while historical records remain auditable.
